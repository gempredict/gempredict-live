# Phase 5L grading economics foundation

The owner-protected Phase 5L Preview retains the saved-report catalog evidence, 30-date lookback and comparable-sales audit. After a sales request, the report explains why grading economics remains unavailable. Use the existing Preview bindings on the immutable deployment for `feature/grading-economics-foundation-phase5l`.

The offline module uses integer minor currency units and selling rates in basis points. It computes proceeds after selling costs, compares incremental cash with the raw-sale alternative, and separately computes accounting profit using acquisition cost on sold outcomes. Break-even uses the same whole-cent fee rounding. Explicitly hypothetical distributions support expected cash outcomes and stress cases. Unsold, returned and grading-failed outcomes model cash and explicit expenses. The value of any retained card is outside the calculation, so those outcomes withhold realized accounting profit.

Grades are keyed by company, scale, number and label. The supported input forms distinguish BGS Black Label, CGC and SGC top labels, PSA half grades where applicable, and TAG whole-number card grades. Legacy labels, qualifiers, unmodeled scale entries and TAG's separate precision score require separate validation. No cross-company conversions are used.

Existing published fee references retain their review date, sources and effective date where supplied. They do not verify checkout pricing. Hypothetical service fees, shipping, insurance, upcharges, resale prices and probabilities do not become market evidence. The existing manual PSA calculator remains available. The new model has no live calculation path, and the readiness receipt exposes blockers without publishing restricted sale values.
