# Atlas Manifesto

## Purpose

Atlas exists to help collectors make better decisions.

It does not replace expertise.

It augments it.

Atlas combines multiple independent evidence sources into one transparent recommendation.

Every recommendation should be explainable.

Every conclusion should be backed by evidence.

Every uncertainty should be communicated honestly.

---

## Principles

### Evidence over opinion

Atlas never guesses when evidence is weak.

---

### Confidence is visible

Every recommendation should include confidence.

---

### Recognition is not intelligence

Identifying a card is only the first step.

Atlas exists to answer:

- Should I grade it?
- Why?
- What are the risks?
- What evidence supports this?
- What could change the recommendation?

---

### Source agnostic

Atlas should work with any vision model.

Any OCR engine.

Any pricing provider.

Any population database.

No vendor should become Atlas.

They are evidence providers.

---

### Transparent

Users should always understand:

- why Atlas reached a conclusion,
- what evidence it used,
- where uncertainty exists.

---

### Learn continuously

Every correction from users makes Atlas better.

Atlas should improve through evidence—not assumptions.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Become the trusted intelligence layer for the trading card industry.

Not another scanner.

Not another price guide.

The decision engine collectors trust.
