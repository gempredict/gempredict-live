# GemPredict Product Charter

## North Star

**GemPredict helps collectors make smarter decisions about their cards. By bringing together card identity, visible condition, real market evidence, grading probabilities, and total costs, GemPredict shows collectors what to grade, sell, hold, or take a closer look at—and why.**

## Product Category

**Trading Card Decision Intelligence**

GemPredict is not primarily a scanner, price guide, AI grader, marketplace, or collection binder.

Those are supporting capabilities.

The product is the recommendation.

## Core Customer

GemPredict is initially built for active collectors, resellers, grading submitters, and small dealers who:

- own more cards than they can efficiently evaluate,
- regularly decide what is worth grading,
- want to avoid wasting submission fees,
- and care about the financial outcome of their decisions.

## First Product Promise

> **GemPredict helps a collector determine the smartest next move for a card before spending money on grading.**

The first version should recommend one of four actions:

- **Grade**
- **Sell Raw**
- **Hold**
- **Inspect Further**

We may keep “Skip” or “Maybe” temporarily in the current interface, but the long-term recommendation system should evolve toward these more actionable outcomes.

## The Seven Questions GemPredict Must Answer

Every completed analysis should work toward answering:

1. What exact card is this?
2. What visible condition evidence can we trust?
3. What condition limitations or risks are present?
4. What does reliable market evidence show?
5. What are the realistic grading outcomes?
6. What will grading actually cost this collector?
7. What is the best next action—and why?

## What Defines GemPredict

GemPredict should be:

- **Decision-first:** Every capability supports an action.
- **Evidence-based:** Recommendations show the underlying identity, condition, market, and cost evidence.
- **Probability-aware:** It models several grading outcomes instead of assuming PSA 10.
- **Cost-aware:** It includes grading, shipping, insurance, selling fees, and opportunity cost where practical.
- **Confidence-transparent:** It distinguishes what is known, inferred, uncertain, or unavailable.
- **Collector-specific:** Recommendations can eventually reflect the collector’s costs, goals, grader, and risk tolerance.
- **Actionable:** It tells the collector what to do next, not merely what the card might be worth.

## What GemPredict Is Not

GemPredict is not trying to become:

- the world’s largest card database,
- the fastest generic card scanner,
- another basic collection tracker,
- another active-listing price guide,
- a card marketplace,
- a replacement for professional grading,
- or a system that pretends photo grading is certain.

## Near-Term Product Scope

The next working version should combine:

- confirmed card identity,
- visible-condition analysis,
- matching market evidence,
- realistic grade probabilities,
- total grading costs,
- raw-versus-graded outcome modeling,
- confidence levels,
- and a clear recommendation.

## Approved Long-Term Expansion

Once the single-card decision engine is trustworthy, GemPredict can expand into:

- What to Grade Next
- bulk card analysis
- submission optimization
- collection opportunity rankings
- Sell, Hold, or Grade monitoring
- dealer inventory prioritization
- actual grading-result tracking
- decision-performance analytics

## Product Filter

Every feature proposal must answer:

> **Does this materially improve GemPredict’s ability to recommend the collector’s smartest next action?**

If the answer is no, it should not interrupt the active roadmap.
